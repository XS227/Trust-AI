How it works

1. Introduce a company by Norwegian organisation number. 2. The business owner confirms sales, costs and commissions. 3. After payment, the commission becomes available for invoicing. 4. Only admins mark payouts as paid.

Standard: 30% of positive net profit after actual costs. Discounted deals can have an approved fixed commission; no payout exceeds profit.
